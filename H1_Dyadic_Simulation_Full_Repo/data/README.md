### Official source and download links
You can download the full dataset (including this CSV and many other related files) directly from the UN Population Division's official website:

Main dataset portal: https://population.un.org/wpp/Download/Standard/CSV/
This is the direct CSV download section. Look for files in the Standard Projections or CSV format group.
The specific file you have (WPP2024_TotalPopulationBySex.csv) is one of the standard outputs containing total population by sex, age group, and year.

Full World Population Prospects 2024 page: https://population.un.org/wpp/
From here, navigate to Download → Standard Projections → CSV format to find the complete set of CSV files.

Summary of Results (with links): https://population.un.org/wpp/Publications/Files/WPP2024_Summary_of_Results.pdf
This PDF links back to the data portal.

Online Data Portal (interactive alternative): https://population.un.org/dataportal/home
You can generate custom extracts (including population by sex) and export as CSV.


### Additional notes

All data is free and publicly available (no login required).
The 2024 revision was released on July 11, 2024.
Files are provided in annual intervals (1-year age groups and time steps) since the 2022 revision.
